What we do

Exterior cleaning services

Every outside surface on a property, cleaned the way that surface needs. From a single drive to a whole commercial site.

Driveway and Patio Cleaning

Block paving, slabs, resin, tarmac and concrete pressure washed back to the colour they were laid in, with the joints re-sanded where they need it.

Roof Cleaning

Moss and lichen removed from tiles and slates without blasting the surface, so the roof drains as it should and looks it from the street.

Soft Washing and Render Cleaning

Low pressure cleaning for render, K-rend and painted walls. Lifts the green and black staining without stripping the finish underneath.

Gutters and Fascia Cleaning

Gutters cleared and running again, and the fascias and soffits cleaned back from grey to white, from the ground with poles wherever possible.

Cladding and Maintenance

Shop fronts, cladding, signage, car parks and forecourts for businesses, landlords and managing agents, worked around your opening hours.

Tennis Courts and Sports Surfaces

Moss, algae and dirt cleared from macadam and artificial courts so the surface plays true and the lines show again.

Why choose us

Cleaned properly, not just blasted

Anyone can hire a pressure washer. The difference is knowing which surfaces can take it and which ones cannot.

The right pressure for the surface

Hard surfaces get the pressure they need. Roofs, render and painted walls get a soft wash instead. Using one setting for everything is how tiles get stripped and render gets scarred.

You can see the difference

Before and after photographs on the work, so you know exactly what was done and what it looked like before we started.
